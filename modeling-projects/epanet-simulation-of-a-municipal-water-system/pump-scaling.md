# Pump Scaling

A 5.5" diameter pump with an initial pump curve was supplied. Scaled pump curves were created by calculating the head and flow coefficients from the given curve, and then using those coefficients as constants to change the diameter to increase flow and head values. A single pump for both the potable and nonpotable system was used as pumps in parallel and series couldn't reach the required head without increasing the cost significantly.



System curves were calculated using the energy equation to solve for a head required from pump value, and corresponding flows and heads were calculated for plotting.
