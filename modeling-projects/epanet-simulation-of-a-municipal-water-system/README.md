# EPANET Simulation of a Municipal Water System

The objectives of this project were to design a neighborhood in AutoCAD on a given plot of land in Irvine, CA, then solve how to pump water to all houses and systems. Required pressures, potable and nonpotable demands, water turnover times, simulated fire demand, tank level, and well outflow limits had to all be met, while accounting for elevation differences and trying to minimize system cost.



Contributions were the EPANET designs for the potable and nonpotable water systems, the pump scaling curves to figure out what combination of pumps to use, contour layouts, and contributions to the group presentation. Group members contributed to demand research, cost analysis, and AutoCAD layout design.
